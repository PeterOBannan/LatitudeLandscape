Build Your Career With Us

Latitude Landscaping is committed to creating a flexible, growth-oriented environment for our team. We don't just build landscapes; we build people. We offer transferable skill training and a highly supportive work experience.

What We Expect

  • Servant Attitude: Recognizing that our work is more than just a job. While you do not need to share our Christian faith, we expect all team members to approach their work with a desire to bless our clients, uplift coworkers, and support their own families through honorable, dedicated labor.
  • Excellence: Taking deep pride in executing every detail to the highest standard.
  • Integrity: Doing the right thing, operating with total transparency, and honoring our commitments, especially when no one is watching.
  • Lifelong Growth: Bringing a self-motivated desire to improve. We want team members who are always looking for the next best way to do things, growing both professionally in the field and personally in their daily lives.
